1. Introduction

JobNestHub ("we", "us", or "our") operates the JobNestHub website and provides an online platform for job seekers and employers.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use JobNestHub, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account information, job applications, cover letters, and information provided when employers post jobs.

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage information.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide job-search and application services, allow employers to manage job postings and applicants, communicate with users, improve the website, maintain security, and comply with applicable laws.

4. Job Applications

When a job seeker submits an application, information associated with the application may be made available to the employer who posted the relevant job.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through job applications or other website forms.

JobNestHub may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain website functionality, and improve the user experience.

Third-party services used by the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by JobNestHub. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

9. Third-Party Websites

JobNestHub may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by third parties.

10. Children's Privacy

JobNestHub is intended for users who are legally permitted to use online employment services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
